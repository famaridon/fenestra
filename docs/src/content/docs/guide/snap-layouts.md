---
title: Snap Layouts
description: Rest the pointer on a window's green button and pick a zone in a palette of layouts, as on Windows 11.
---

As on Windows 11, resting the pointer on a window's **green button** (the zoom button, top left of the window) opens a palette of **layouts**. Click a zone in one of them: the window snaps there.

## How it works

1. Move the pointer over the green button of a window and leave it there for a moment.
2. A palette appears under the button. Each thumbnail is your display split into zones.
3. **Click** a zone: the window takes that place on its display and comes to the front.

Moving the pointer away from the button and the palette, **Escape** or a click anywhere else closes the palette.

After a half or a quarter, [Snap Assist](/fenestra/guide/snap-assist/) offers your other windows for the remaining space, as after any other snap.

## Layouts offered

The palette follows the **orientation of the display** the window is on:

| Display | Layouts |
|---|---|
| Landscape | two halves; a half and two quarters (both ways); four quarters; three thirds; two thirds and one third (both ways); full screen |
| [Vertical](/fenestra/guide/vertical-display/) | top and bottom halves; a half and two quarters (both ways); four quarters; full screen |

A layout only appears when **all its zones are enabled** in the **Zones** tab of the [settings](/fenestra/guide/settings/). Thirds are off by default, so their layouts only show up once you turn them on.

## Turning it off

Snap Layouts can be turned off in the **General** tab of the [settings](/fenestra/guide/settings/).

:::note
macOS shows its own menu when the pointer stays on the green button. Fenestra's palette opens first and covers it.
:::
